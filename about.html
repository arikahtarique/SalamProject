Why visit Pakistan

From the world's second highest peak to Mughal gardens and ancient cities.

Mountains

Home to K2 and the Karakoram, Himalaya and Hindu Kush ranges.

Mughal heritage

Lahore Fort, Badshahi Mosque and Shalimar Gardens.

Valleys

Hunza, Swat, Skardu and Neelum, known for lakes and orchards.

History

Mohenjo-daro, a major city of the Indus Valley Civilization, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Food

From Karachi biryani to Lahori chargha and Peshawari chapli kebab, every region has its own flavours.

Culture

Music, truck art, handicrafts and festivals make travel here colourful and memorable.
